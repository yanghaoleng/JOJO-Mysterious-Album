# Landing artwork, September 2026

`hero`, `candy`, `chapter-*`, and `concept-*` are original procedural 3D concept renders from `src/landing-scene.js`, reusing the project's existing `/dev/models.js` clay characters. They illustrate the homepage direction; they are not captures of released chapter gameplay. The three journey examples use fictional aliases and authored sample text.

`history-story`, `history-lab`, `studio`, `friends`, `yellow-four`, and `iphone-duo` are actual local product screenshots captured by `tools/capture-landing.mjs`. The iPhone Duo screenshot is captured after expanding the device. Existing product and third-party asset notices continue to apply.

All images are local WebP. Regenerate with `tools/capture-landing.mjs` after starting the project server; see its header for optional runtime paths. `tools/landing-art.html` is only the deterministic capture surface.
